# Payments

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal, and Apple Pay. All prices are in US dollars.

## When you are charged

Online orders are charged when the parcel ships. A temporary authorization hold appears on your card when the order is placed and is released automatically.

## Failed payments

If a payment fails, the order is held for 48 hours. Update the payment method from the order page within that window or the order is cancelled.

## Sales tax

Sales tax is calculated at checkout based on the delivery address. Tax-exempt organizations can email a certificate to our support team for a refund of tax already charged.
