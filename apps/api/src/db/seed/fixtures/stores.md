# Stores

Northwind Outfitters has stores in Portland, Denver, Chicago, and Boston. Store hours are 10 am to 8 pm Monday to Saturday and 11 am to 6 pm on Sunday.

## Returns in store

Online orders can be returned at any store with the order confirmation email. Refunds are issued to the original payment method.

## Store pickup

Choose pickup at checkout to collect an online order at a store. Pickup orders are held for 7 days. Bring a photo ID and the order number.

## Finding a store

Use the store locator on our website to see addresses, hours, and current stock. Stock levels shown online are updated every 30 minutes.
