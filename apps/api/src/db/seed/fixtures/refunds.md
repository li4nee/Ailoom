# Refunds

Northwind Outfitters accepts returns within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. Items must be unworn, unwashed, and have their original tags attached.

## Annual plan purchases

Annual membership plans are refundable in full within 14 days of purchase. After 14 days, annual plans are refundable on a pro-rata basis for the unused months.

## How refunds are issued

Refunds go back to the original payment method. Card refunds appear within 5 to 10 business days. Cash and store-credit purchases are refunded as store credit unless you ask for cash at the counter.

## Sale items

Items bought on final sale are not refundable, but they can be exchanged for another size within 30 days.
