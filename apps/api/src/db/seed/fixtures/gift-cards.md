# Gift cards

Northwind gift cards are available from 25 to 500 dollars. They can be used online and in stores.

## Balance and expiry

Check your balance on the gift card page with the card number and PIN. Gift cards do not expire and have no monthly fees.

## Lost cards

Treat a gift card like cash. If it is lost or stolen, report it with the card number and we can freeze the remaining balance and issue a replacement.

## Combining with a purchase

You can use more than one gift card on a single order. Any remaining balance stays on the card for the next purchase.
