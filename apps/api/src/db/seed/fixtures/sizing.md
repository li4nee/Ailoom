# Sizing

Our jackets use US sizing. A size Medium fits a 38 to 40 inch chest. Between sizes, choose the larger size for a relaxed fit.

## Measuring

Measure around the fullest part of your chest with the tape level and snug. For trousers, measure your natural waist just above the hip bones.

## Fit guide

Regular fit leaves room for a sweater. Slim fit is close to the body and runs one size small. Relaxed fit is the widest cut.

## Kids

Kids sizes are listed by age and height in centimeters. If a child is between two ages, choose the larger size.
