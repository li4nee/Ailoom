# Shipping

Standard shipping is free on orders over 75 dollars and costs 6 dollars otherwise. Standard orders arrive in 3 to 5 business days.

## Express shipping

Express shipping costs 15 dollars and arrives in 1 to 2 business days. Express orders placed before 2 pm ship the same day.

## International shipping

We ship to Canada and the European Union. International delivery takes 7 to 14 business days and does not include duties or taxes.

## Tracking

Every order receives a tracking link by email once the parcel leaves our warehouse. Tracking updates can take up to 24 hours to appear.
