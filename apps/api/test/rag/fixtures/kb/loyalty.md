# Loyalty

The Northwind Trail club earns one point for every dollar spent. Points are worth one cent each when redeemed on an order.

## Tiers

Trail members earn 1 point per dollar. Summit members, who spend 1,000 dollars a year, earn 1.5 points per dollar. Ridge members, who spend 3,000 dollars a year, earn 2 points per dollar and free express shipping.

## Redeeming points

Redeem points at checkout in increments of 500. Points never expire while your account is active.

## Exclusions

Points are not earned on gift card purchases, taxes, or shipping fees. Points earned on an order are reversed if the order is returned.
