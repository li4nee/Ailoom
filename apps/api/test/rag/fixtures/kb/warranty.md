# Warranty

Every Northwind jacket carries a two-year warranty against manufacturing defects. The warranty covers stitching, zippers, and seam failure.

## What is not covered

Normal wear, accidental tears, stains, and damage from improper washing are not covered. Alterations by a third party void the warranty.

## Claims

Start a warranty claim from your account page and upload a photo of the defect and your order number. Claims are reviewed within 5 business days. Approved claims are repaired or replaced at no cost.

## Repairs

Out-of-warranty repairs are available for a fee. Send the item to our repair center and we will quote the cost before starting work.
