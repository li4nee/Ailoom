# Accounts

Create a Northwind account with an email address and password. An account lets you track orders, save addresses, and start warranty claims.

## Resetting a password

Choose Forgot password on the sign-in page. A reset link is emailed and expires after one hour. If the email does not arrive, check the spam folder.

## Changing your email

Change the email on your account page. We send a confirmation link to the new address before the change takes effect.

## Deleting an account

You can delete your account from the privacy settings page. Order records are kept for accounting for seven years, but your profile and saved addresses are removed immediately.
