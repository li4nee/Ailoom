## One support workflow

<ol class="docs-workflow">
  <li>
    <strong>Prepare your knowledge</strong>
    <p>Add help pages, files, or text. Test what the bot retrieves before publishing.</p>
  </li>
  <li>
    <strong>Answer with evidence</strong>
    <p>Visitors get knowledge-backed replies. Your team can inspect sources and model usage.</p>
  </li>
  <li>
    <strong>Bring in your team</strong>
    <p>Hand off to a shared inbox, where agents continue in the same widget thread.</p>
  </li>
</ol>
